Yes. We help you identify and close on replacement property within your 45-day and 180-day deadlines, including industrial condo units we own and sell. We work alongside your CPA and qualified intermediary. We do not provide tax or legal advice.

I'm a tenant. How do I request maintenance?

Sign in to the tenant portal to submit a request. For emergencies, call (801) 210-0170.

What is CAM?

Common area maintenance. It covers the shared costs of running a property, such as landscaping, snow removal, parking lot upkeep, and shared utilities. Under an NNN lease, each tenant pays a share based on the space they lease.
